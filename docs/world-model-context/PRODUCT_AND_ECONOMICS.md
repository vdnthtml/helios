# Product, customers, sources, and economics

Messaging is the preferred first interaction. A customer asks a question, receives an investigation/job reference, and gets an evidence-grounded answer with relevant relationships, explanation, uncertainty, potential developments, sources, and missing information. They can inspect permitted sources and continue the investigation. A graph visualization is optional.

One backend should serve future messaging adapters, MCP, CLI, APIs, enterprise tools, and monitoring. Candidate operations are investigate/get investigation, resolve entity, trace relationship, get evidence/hypotheses, compare history, create/resolve forecast, create monitor, submit research, and export context. These are conceptual operations, not implemented endpoints.

| Opportunity | Value to validate |

| Personal intelligence | Portable research context and useful monitoring at an affordable cost |
| Market intelligence | Company, capital, supplier, competitor, and downstream relationship research |

Select the first buyer using urgency, existing spending, accessible contacts, usable historical data, speed to demonstrable value, and sales complexity. Seek a genuine financial commitment. No pricing, revenue, paid pilot, or customer segment has been validated in the supplied material. Subscriptions, usage plans, enterprise contracts, API metering, and paid investigations are possibilities.

Candidate information categories: official filings and corporate registries; transactions, procurement, supply chains, and market data; papers, patents, citations, and technology documentation; permitted news/social/publication data; authorized transcripts and private company documents; original interviews and licensed specialized datasets. For every candidate source verify historical depth, provenance, access, costs, rate limits, commercial reuse, retention/training restrictions, export, and quality.

Names mentioned in historical discussion, such as SEC, GLEIF, Companies House, OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar, Bluesky, and Google APIs, are research leads only. Their present capabilities, permissions, and prices have not been checked in this documentation task. Competitor and funding claims in older assistant text are likewise unverified here.

Operate locally with low infrastructure commitment initially; team capacity, current cash, hardware, funding eligibility, and available contacts need fresh confirmation. Earlier resource descriptions and one-month ambitions are historical context, not a verified budget or delivery promise.

Track acquisition, extraction, investigation, forecast, storage, model, and human-review costs. Incremental processing, caching, task-specific model routing, and stopping policies must be evaluated against evidence quality. A small selective team and public useful research can coexist with confidential implementation and customer records.
