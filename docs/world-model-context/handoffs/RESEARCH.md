# Specialist research handoff

## Objective

Find the smallest technically coherent architecture that can test the broad information-world-model hypothesis. Challenge assumptions and recommend components using primary evidence. This packet authorizes research within the current assigned task; it is not an instruction to deploy, contact people, or collect private data.

## Required inputs

Read `../CURRENT_DIRECTION.md`, `../DECISIONS.md`, `../reference/RESEARCH_MISSION.md` (complete research instructions), and `../reference/ARCHITECTURE_REQUIREMENTS.md` (complete capability scope). Read `../EVALUATION.md`, `../SECURITY.md`, and `../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

## Coverage

Preserve all source workstreams A–W: ontology; identity; temporal memory; provenance; storage/graphs; segmentation; GraphRAG/retrieval; logic/hypotheses; attribution/causality; decision frameworks; economic intelligence; narratives; forecasting; models/training; data sources; incremental data engineering; autonomous workflows; security; interfaces; competition; economics/deployment; evaluation; founder research and portability. Discover missing disciplines if needed.

Research mature and experimental approaches. Separate direct fits, adjacent components, and speculative research. Verify current repository URLs, license text, maintenance dates, implemented capabilities, dependencies, local feasibility, security/production limits, benchmarks, and integration gaps. Distinguish inspected code/docs, published research, vendor claims, inference, and unknowns. Cite primary sources and check dates. Do not invent capabilities or assume popularity equals suitability.

## Required output

Deliver the original 15 requested artifacts: executive verdict; research landscape; repository atlas; technology comparison; requirement gap analysis; candidate stack and alternatives; potential original research; permissible data-source catalog; benchmark plan; minimal prototype; scaling architecture; risks; build/reuse matrix; dependency roadmap; unresolved decisions.

Compare at least three architectures: minimal local, research-oriented, and production-oriented. For each show components/flow, storage, inference, access, updates, evaluation compatibility, bottlenecks, costs, tradeoffs, and migration. Map each recommendation to requirements, with disposition Reuse / Integrate / Extend / Build / Research / Defer / Reject and a reason.

## Acceptance

Coverage is traceable to the full register; serious technology candidates have verifiable evidence; alternatives and gaps remain explicit; benchmarks could falsify the claimed advantage; the minimum prototype exercises inference, historical prediction, feedback, evidence, time, cross-domain retrieval, and budgets. Proposed changes must identify affected requirements and tradeoffs. Do not silently reduce the vision to finance, a chatbot, or a graph database.

Log findings in `../logs/RESEARCH.jsonl`, recommendations as proposals in `../DECISIONS.md`, and update state/changelog. A new research task has not been executed merely because this handoff exists.
